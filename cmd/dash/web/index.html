Five quorumd processes on one machine, each with its own write-ahead log, talking over links that can be cut. Several clients read, write, append and compare-and-set against them the whole time, and their history is checked for linearizability ten seconds at a time. Break it: kill nodes, cut the leader off, split the network. The cluster is shared with everyone looking at this page, and puts itself back together after 30 s left alone.

Linearizability

Each square is ten seconds of history. The workload moves to fresh keys each window, so a window can be checked on its own once its last operation has answered or given up. Operations that gave up count as may have happened: the checker must find an order that works either way.
